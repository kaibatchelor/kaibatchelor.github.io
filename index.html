About Me

At Williams, I chair the Economics Student Advisory Committee and am beginning to explore finance through research projects. I’m drawn to the chance to learn alongside smart, hardworking people who push me to improve. Security analysis and valuation projects are next on my list.

Swimming is a big part of my life, but some of my favorite hours at the pool have been spent teaching. In high school, I volunteered at my local pool and taught swim lessons, something I found especially rewarding.

In summer 2026, I spent two months in Japan studying Japanese, visiting family, and training with a swim team. Exploring Tokyo was also a chance to reconnect with a part of my culture I’d lost touch with.

Outside school and swimming, I love making music, whether with friends or on my own. I play violin, guitar, banjo, erhu, and shamisen, plus a little piano. I also enjoy bass fishing, hiking, golf, and weightlifting.
